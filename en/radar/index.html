The method

The rules were written on 17 October 2026 and completed on 18 October, before the final ranking and before any release was published (role-relative scoring and quotas). From now on any change creates a new dated version and applies only to later picks.

Three conditions to get in

  • at least 35% of available minutes;
  • numbers confirmed by at least two independent sources;
  • born in 2003 or later.

How the score is built

  • 45Output in the role, corrected for small samples, as a percentile within the role
  • 25Share of minutes played
  • 15Age relative to the under-age quota
  • 15Team's league position

The small-sample correction adds three “average” matches for the role to every player, so a few good games are not enough to reach the top.

A pick counts as a hit if at season's end it holds its minutes (−10 points tolerance) and its output is above the role median, or if the player moves up a level or gets a representative call-up. I publish the hit rate, misses included, against a naive baseline: the three under-age players with the most minutes in the group.

Limits

  • There is no official source for player minutes in Serie D. Minutes come from two unofficial sources, Transfermarkt and zerozero.
  • Minutes confidence is “high” only when match reports cover every match; otherwise it is “medium”.
  • For a centre-back the score uses starts and the team's goals conceded per match: a team-level figure, because goals conceded with him on the pitch are not public.
  • The score compares each player with his own role: with groups of 7-9 eligible players the percentiles are imprecise, and a 60 for a centre-back is not the same as a 60 for a forward.
  • The role averages used in the correction are stated assumptions. They will be recomputed on the whole group at the first check.

What this means for a club

In Serie D data is produced every week, but almost nobody reads it with a method. My work is closing that gap, with rules anyone can check.
